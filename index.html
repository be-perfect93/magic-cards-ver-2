1-Bet mode — every move is its own bet and pays out on the spot.

How to Play — 1-Bet

  • 💰 Coin cards are worth 1×–1.5× your bet (A) and pay A × multiplier B
  • Every row = 1 bet, charged when the row is revealed (Start, and every new row). Pick a card → it pays A × B right away. Ending the run doesn't refund a revealed row
  • A = the card's base value (coins, monster loot, portal coins). B = the multiplier of the step you land on
  • Paid wins go straight to your wallet and are never multiplied again
  • 🏔️ Every 5 moves is a summit: big multiplier jump. Max HP is always 10
  • ⚡ Multiplier: every ladder step +10% (stacks)
  • ⏫ Next-Row Boost: the next row multiplier ×2 (that row only)
  • 💚 Heal restores HP. 🐉 Monster shows its damage; one in your lane ambushes you first and CAN kill you. ☠ locked if damage ≥ your HP
  • 🚀 Cash Boost: distance revealed after you pick — 1 to 3 steps. Every row crossed pays a random coin × that row's B — all for ONE bet
  • 🐉 Monsters hit for up to 9 HP — anything 10+ is a 💀 Reaper
  • 💀 Reaper blocks its lane. Die → get pushed down one step on the ladder (e.g. 2.40x → 2.20x), lose all boosts and restart with 5 HP. Never below the first rung
  • 🏆 Reach step 20 without dying: Flawless Jackpot 25× bet
